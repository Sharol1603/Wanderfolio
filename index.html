Prologue

It began with a suitcase and a question.

What if the world is simply bigger and kinder than I'd been told? So I packed light, kept my eyes wide open, and went to find out. These are the pages I wrote along the way — in temples, on trains, over coffee, in the quiet after fireworks.

— the Traveler
